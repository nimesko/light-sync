# Code of conduct

LightSync is a small personal project. Anyone who takes part (issues, pull requests, discussions) is
expected to:

- Be kind and patient. People of every background and experience level are welcome.
- Talk about the code and the ideas, not the person.
- Accept that the maintainer may say no to a change, and why.
- Respect privacy: never post someone else's personal information, and don't ask people to share
  logs, screenshots or files that show more than the problem needs.

Harassment, insults, sexual or discriminatory language, and trolling are not accepted, in public or in
private messages about the project.

If something here goes wrong, report it privately through GitHub's **Security > Report a
vulnerability** on this repository, or by a private message to the maintainer on GitHub. Reports are
kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and block people who don't
follow this code.
